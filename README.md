# Unheard

Where was this filmed? Drop a video or a sound file: the planes in its background leave a tone
that falls as they pass, and matched to an archive of flights those tones can sometimes tell where
and when it was recorded. Or hold your phone up to the sky for two minutes: Unheard finds the
faint hum of an aircraft and names it, from its Doppler curve and live ADS-B flight data.

Everything runs in your browser. Your recording never leaves your phone unless you choose to
donate it.

Try it on a phone: https://unheard.gwendall.com

Made by Gwendall.

## How it works

1. **Listen.** The page records raw audio (no echo cancellation, no noise suppression) and polls
   the aircraft around a coarse version of your location every 3 s (`/api/aircraft`, adsb.lol).
2. **Model.** For every aircraft, `lib/core` rebuilds its track (Kalman/RTS smoothing of the
   ADS-B fixes), then computes the exact pitch curve you should have heard: emission time solved
   for each moment (the sound left the aircraft seconds earlier), radial speed at that moment,
   speed of sound from the temperature, wind if known.
3. **Match.** The recording becomes an evidence map at four window lengths (2 s to 0.25 s; each
   point of a curve is read where its slope is best resolved). Each aircraft's curve is swept over
   its unknown tone (40 Hz to 2 kHz) and the phone's clock error (±2 s), scored against its own
   wrong guesses, and the best aircraft wins if it stands clear of the others.

One function, `analyze()` in `lib/core/pipeline.ts`, is the whole analysis for the page, the
tests and the evals. What the evals measure is what runs in production.

## What it is not

Unheard names the plane above the place where you stand. It does not, and will not, search for
where a recording was made. The inverse problem (sweep the listener's position until a curve
fits) is a short step from this code, and the method has been public since
[colsto's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UytYrhYLOKI). We do not ship it, and donated
recordings are anonymised before they leave the phone: the listener sits at the origin of a
local frame, with no latitude or longitude, no absolute time and no aircraft identifier.

## Run

```sh
pnpm install
pnpm dev               # then open the printed URL; ?capture=1 adds a "Download recording bundle" button
pnpm test              # unit tests
SEEDS=10 pnpm eval evals/synthetic   # a quick eval, about a minute
```

## Tests and evals

| Layer | What | Command | Where it runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unit | FFT, Doppler physics, tracks, matcher on synthetic scenes | `pnpm test` | CI |
| Synthetic eval | 66+ made-up scenes: faint, far, windy, crowded sky, plane missing from the data | `pnpm eval evals/synthetic` | CI (ratchet) |
| Real tracks | real ADS-B recorded with `pnpm record-sky`, made-up sound, half the polls | `pnpm eval evals/real-tracks` | CI (ratchet) |
| Real audio | AeroSonicDB recordings + ADS-B Exchange history, Livermore phone recordings + their ADS-B tracks, and the page's own capture bundles | `node scripts/fetch-aerosonic.ts && node scripts/fetch-livermore.ts && pnpm eval evals/real-audio` | on demand (data not in git) |
| E2E | the real page in Chromium, fake microphone playing a fixture, replayed ADS-B | `BASE_URL=<url> pnpm e2e` | CI |

See `evals/README.md` for the metrics, the baseline and how to add real recordings.

## Contribute

You can help without writing code: record a flyby and donate it. Or add a city's sky, an
aircraft's sound, a better matcher, or build a listening station. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), and [docs/OPEN-SOURCE.md](docs/OPEN-SOURCE.md) for the plan.
